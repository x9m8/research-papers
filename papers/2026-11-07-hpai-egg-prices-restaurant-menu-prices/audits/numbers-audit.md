## FLAGGED — not traceable within the package / caveats

14. The short-sample caveats the manuscript itself raises are real and should be read as substantive, not pro forma: the layer-to-price analysis has only 45–56 monthly observations (3 HPAI waves) and the reduced-form check has 13 overlapping horizons with no multiple-comparisons correction — the manuscript's own Limitations §4.3 ("I did not correct for the number of horizons and specifications examined... the confidence intervals are probably somewhat optimistic") is accurate and should not be weakened in any edit.
15. No arithmetic or regression-specification errors were found anywhere I could rerun the code. All CI/point-estimate/p-value numbers in the abstract, §3.1, §3.2, and Tables 2–5 trace cleanly to `results/*.json` and, on rerun, to `data/raw/*`.

No numbers were found that fail to trace, and no instance of asserted (rather than computed) statistics was found apart from item 13.
