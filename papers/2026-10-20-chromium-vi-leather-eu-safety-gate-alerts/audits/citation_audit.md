# Citation audit: "Fewer alerts, same allergy: hexavalent chromium in EU leather goods, 2005–2026"

Auditor: independent check (Claude, claude-opus-5-5), 20 October 2026. I did not write the manuscript. Every source below was re-opened for this audit. The literature brief (/home/claude/p4/lit/brief.md) was used only to find URLs.

Access notes: curl to api.crossref.org was blocked by the sandbox proxy (403). Crossref was read through WebFetch (two records) and then through the user's Chrome (fetch from an api.crossref.org page). One WebFetch call to Crossref was rate limited (429). Europe PMC REST was read through Chrome. The RAC/SEAC opinion page showed an Azure WAF challenge in Chrome; I did not try to pass it and read the document through WebFetch instead. No cookies were accepted and no forms were submitted.

Basis key: **FT** = full text read; **FT-part** = relevant section of full text read; **Abs** = abstract or metadata only; **Sec** = second-hand (a source quoting another).

## C. Search for existing analyses of Safety Gate/RAPEX Cr(VI) leather notifications

Re-run on 20 Oct 2026:
- Europe PMC: `(RAPEX OR "Safety Gate" OR "rapid alert system") AND (chromium OR chromate) AND leather` returned 3 hits, none relevant: a cornflakes metal study, Krogh Pedersen et al. 2025 on skin-sensitiser risk assessment (*Contact Dermatitis* 92:446–459), and a leather biocide study.
- Crossref bibliographic query "RAPEX chromium leather notifications" returned general RAPEX papers and ISO 17075 standards. None counts Cr(VI) leather notifications.
- Web searches (standard and extended) found trade press, Certifico weekly-report mirrors, the Austrian BMK RAPEX page (19 of 72 Austrian chemical RAPEX cases in 2015 were Cr(VI) in leather) and a new general analysis, Pigłowski (2026) *Sustainability* 18(13):6875 (44,330 alerts 2005–2025). Pigłowski 2026 does not count Cr(VI) leather alerts.
- One grey-literature item could not be checked. Search results list a CTC (French leather and footwear technical centre) post, "Europe 2019: summary of product recalls for chemical non-compliances (RAPEX Safety Gate)", 18 Feb 2020. The URL now redirects to the CTC blog index, so its content is unknown. It probably tallied 2019 chemical recalls, likely including Cr(VI) in leather. Scopus and Web of Science were not available.

Conclusion: no peer-reviewed count or trend analysis was found. The claim is supported if worded "no peer-reviewed study has counted or analysed…". Industry bodies may have published annual tallies, so "as far as I can find, no study" should be narrowed to peer-reviewed work, with a note on trade summaries.
